ew Workspace Bridge

A personal command-line assistant that connects to Gmail and Google Drive.

What it is

ew Workspace Bridge is a private, single-user tool. It runs on the owner's own computer and connects to the owner's own Google account through the Gmail and Drive APIs. It is used to read, search and summarise the owner's own messages and files from a terminal.

Who can use it

One person: the account owner. The application is not distributed, not sold, and has no other users.

What it does

Read messages, search the mailbox, read files from Drive, and draft or send messages when the owner explicitly asks for it. Nothing happens in the background, on a schedule, or on someone else's account.
